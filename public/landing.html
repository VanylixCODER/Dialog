Questions

Fair questions.

Is it end-to-end encrypted?

Calls go directly between participants, and Secret chats are end-to-end encrypted. Regular messages are encrypted in transit and stored on the server so they sync between your devices.

What does the server know about me?

Your username, a hashed password, your regular messages and what's needed to deliver them. No phone number, no address book, no analytics.

How big can a call get?

Calls are peer to peer, so everyone sends to everyone. Small groups work best; huge rooms aren't what it's built for.

Is it free?

Yes. No paid tier, no limits on calls or screen share, and the code is open source.
